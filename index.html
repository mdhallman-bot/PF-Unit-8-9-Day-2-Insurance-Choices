2 · Health plans

Low monthly price or lower risk?

A premium buys coverage but normally does not count toward your deductible or out-of-pocket maximum.
Plan termPlan GPlan H

Illustrative individual plans. Assume all services are covered and in-network, no copays, no prior spending, and the annual out-of-pocket caps apply to this episode. A cap covers patient cost sharing, not premiums.

Healthy year

There are no covered medical bills this year. Calculate each plan's total annual spending.

Hospitalization year

A covered in-network hospital stay has a allowed amount of $21,000. Calculate total annual premiums plus patient medical cost.

Method: annual premium = monthly premium × 12. Patient share = deductible + coinsurance on remaining allowed charges, limited by the out-of-pocket maximum. Annual total = premium + patient share.
